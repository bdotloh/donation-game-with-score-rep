\section{Score dynamics: from strategies and a norm to stable score distributions}\label{ch:scores}

\subsection{Population}\label{sec:population}\emph{module} \code{engine/population.py}

\subsubsection{\texttt{Population} and \texttt{score\_array}}

\whatit
\code{Population} is a list of strategies with their frequencies $\rho$. \code{score\_array} turns the \emph{initial} score
distributions into the array $w$ that the score dynamics work on.

\howit
\code{Population} checks that $\rho$ has one non-negative entry per strategy and sums to 1 (equal frequencies by
default). \code{score\_array} accepts either one distribution, shared by all $n$ strategies, or one per strategy,
pads them with zeros to a common length $L$ and checks that each is non-negative and sums to 1; it returns the
$n\times L$ array \code{w[i, j]}.

\paramsh
\begin{params}
strategies & list of \code{Strategy} & the strategies; at least one.\\
rho & array of $n$ floats, default equal & the frequencies; must sum to 1.\\
w\_init & 1-D or 2-D array-like & \code{score\_array(w\_init, n)}: one distribution, or one row per strategy.\\
\end{params}
Attributes: \code{strategies}, \code{rho}, \code{n}.

\subsection{Transition rates}\label{sec:rates}\emph{module} \code{engine/rates.py}

\subsubsection{\texttt{role\_factor}}

\whatit
The probability $\kappa=q(1-q)$ that a given ordered pair of roles (this individual Can, the other Need) occurs, when each
individual can help with probability $q$. \code{ROLE\_FACTOR}${}=0.25$ is the value for $q=\tfrac12$.

\paramsh
\begin{params}
q & float in $(0,1)$ & the probability that an individual can help. \code{ValueError} otherwise.\\
\end{params}

\subsubsection{\texttt{TransitionRates} and \texttt{Rates}}

\whatit
Given a population and its current score distributions, computes the rates at which a score of each strategy moves up and
down: $G,F$ from interactions alone, and $g,f$ with noise. Returns them in a \code{Rates} record.

\howit
Take an individual of strategy $i$ with score $s$. Let $x(\cdot)$ be a score's class under the norm, and let
\[
 W_c=\text{mass of the population in class } c,\qquad
 B_{c,x}=\sum_k\rho_k\sum_{d\in x} w^{(k)}_d\,h_k(c,d)
\]
(the mass of Cans in norm class $x$ who help a Need of class $c$), and $M_x=\sum_k\rho_k\sum_{d\in x}w^{(k)}_d$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{As the Can}, it meets a Need of class $c$ with probability $W_c$ and helps with probability $h_i(c,s)$. Its
score goes up with probability
$U^{\mathrm{can}}_s=\sum_c W_c\bigl[(1-h_i(c,s))\,[CN_{c,0,x(s)}=+1]+h_i(c,s)\,[CN_{c,1,x(s)}=+1]\bigr]$ and down with $D^{\mathrm{can}}_s$,
the same with $-1$.
\item \emph{As the Need}, in class $a=x(s)$, it meets a Can of class $x$ who helps with total weight $B_{a,x}$ and declines
with weight $M_x-B_{a,x}$:
$U^{\mathrm{need}}_s=\sum_x\bigl[(M_x-B_{a,x})\,[NC_{a,0,x}=+1]+B_{a,x}\,[NC_{a,1,x}=+1]\bigr]$, and $D^{\mathrm{need}}_s$ with $-1$.
\end{itemize}
The rates are the role factor times the sums:
\[
 G^{(i)}_{s+1}=\kappa\,(U^{\mathrm{can}}_s+U^{\mathrm{need}}_s),\qquad
 F^{(i)}_{s}=\kappa\,(D^{\mathrm{can}}_s+D^{\mathrm{need}}_s),
\]
and with noise $g=(1-\varepsilon)G+\varepsilon\varepsilon_\uparrow$, $f=(1-\varepsilon)F+\varepsilon\varepsilon_\downarrow$
(with $g_0=f_0=0$). The code does not loop over scores and classes: it works on the coarsest partition of the scores that
refines the norm's classes and every strategy's receiver classes, so each call is a few array operations whatever the
strategies are, and the class tables are cached per set of strategies.

\paramsh
\begin{params}
norm & \code{ScoringNorm} & the update rule.\\
noise & \code{Noise} & the noise.\\
role\_factor & float, \code{0.25} & $\kappa$ (see \code{role\_factor(q)}).\\
population & \code{Population} & the call \code{rates(population, w)}.\\
w & array $(n,L)$ & the current score distributions; the top score should be empty (see \code{chain.extend}).\\
\end{params}
The returned \code{Rates} holds \code{G} and \code{g} of shape $(n,L+1)$ and \code{F} and \code{f} of shape $(n,L)$.

\subsection{The birth--death chain}\label{sec:chain}\emph{module} \code{engine/chain.py}

\subsubsection{\texttt{extend}, \texttt{change}, \texttt{stationary}}

\whatit
\code{change} gives the change of the score distributions in one step of the chain, \code{extend} makes room for mass to
move up, and \code{stationary} gives the stationary distribution of a chain whose rates do not change.

\howit
With the rates of section~\ref{sec:rates}, the change of $w_j$ in one step is
\[
 \Delta w_j=-w_j\,(g_{j+1}+f_j)+w_{j-1}\,g_j+w_{j+1}\,f_{j+1}
\]
(outflow up and down, inflow from below and from above), which sums to zero. It needs the top score to be empty so
that the up-flow has somewhere to go: \code{extend} appends an empty score when the top one has mass. For constant
rates detailed balance gives the stationary distribution $\pi_j\propto\prod_{s=1}^{j} g_s/f_s$, computed with logarithms
for stability and normalised.

\paramsh
\begin{params}
w & array $(n,L)$ & score distributions (\code{change} needs \code{w[:, -1]} to be 0).\\
g & array $(n,L+1)$ & up-rates, \code{g[:, j]} from $j-1$ to $j$.\\
f & array $(n,L)$ & down-rates, \code{f[:, j]} from $j$ to $j-1$.\\
g, f & 1-D arrays of equal length $S+1$ & \code{stationary(g, f)}: all entries from index 1 positive.\\
\end{params}

\subsection{Stable score distributions}\label{sec:solver}\emph{module} \code{engine/solver.py}

\subsubsection{\texttt{StableScoreSolver} and \texttt{StableScores}}

\whatit
Finds the stable score distribution of every strategy in a population, by running all the score chains together until
they stop changing. Returns a \code{StableScores} record.

\howit
Because each chain's rates depend on every strategy's distribution, the chains are stepped together:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Start from \code{w = score\_array(w\_init)}.
\item Repeat up to \code{max\_iter} times: \code{extend} $w$; compute the rates from it; add the change
($w\leftarrow w+\Delta w$, one unit of time); renormalise each row to sum 1; if \code{tail\_tol}${}>0$ drop the top
scores whose mass is below it in every chain.
\item Stop early when $\max|\Delta w|\le$\code{tol} (\emph{converged}), or when every chain has lost all its mass in its initial
scores (\emph{transient}: the chain has left where it started and keeps spreading), unless \code{force\_max\_iter}.
\end{enumerate}
The score range grows as needed; there is no maximum score. At the end the solver checks for \emph{drift}: above the
highest class cutoff the rates no longer change with the score, so if the up-rate at the top score is at least the
down-rate there is no stable distribution, however small the last change was. Then \code{converged} is false.
The record keeps \code{w} (the result), \code{w\_prev} (the distributions the last rates were computed from) and
\code{rates} (those rates).

\paramsh
\begin{params}
norm & \code{ScoringNorm} & the update rule.\\
noise & \code{Noise}, default \code{Noise()} & the noise.\\
tol & float, \code{1e-5} & converged when the largest change in one step is at most this.\\
max\_iter & int, \code{10000} & the most steps.\\
stop\_when\_transient & bool, \code{True} & stop when every chain has left its initial scores.\\
force\_max\_iter & bool, \code{False} & never stop early: always take \code{max\_iter} steps.\\
record\_history & bool, \code{False} & keep \code{(w, rates)} after every step in \code{history}.\\
role\_factor & float, \code{0.25} & $\kappa$.\\
tail\_tol & float, \code{0.0} & if positive, drop top scores with mass below it (keeps long runs short).\\
population, w\_init & & the call \code{solver(population, w\_init)}.\\
\end{params}
\code{solver.with\_options(**options)} returns a copy with \code{tol}, \code{max\_iter}, \dots{} changed (not the norm, noise
or role factor). The result has: \code{w} $(n\times L)$, \code{w0} (the fraction with score 0), \code{w\_prev}, \code{rates},
\code{n\_iter}, \code{converged}, \code{transient}, \code{drifting} (one flag per strategy), \code{history}.
