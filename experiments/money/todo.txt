money norm means population of rational agents who exchange scores for help. 
exchange happens when one in need meets one who can.
one in need gains b (in terms of fitness) and loses 1 score. 
one who cans loses c (in terms of fitness) and gains 1 score. 

under the following assumptions, the score distribution has an equilibrium that is geometric.

the following assumptions:
0. a large an unstructured unpopulation. 
The chance of meeting an agent of a certain type is given by the proportion of that type in the population
1. everyone who has at least a score has some chance of losing one. everyone has some chance of gaining a score.
 the chance of losing one is more than the chance of gaining one

let r be the ratio between the chance of gaining (\epsilon_\up) and losing a score (\epsilon_\down). by assumption 1, r <1.
let \pi_j be the proportion of the population with j score at the equilibrium distribution.
\pi_0 = (1-r)
\pi_j = r^j \pi_0 = r^j (1-r)

the helping rate under the money norm is (1-\pi_0)^2 = (1-(1-r))^2 = r^2
